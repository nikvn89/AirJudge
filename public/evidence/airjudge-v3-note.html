AirJudge: understanding the pool, reserved rewards and creator reclaim

Public contribution note for campaign airjudge-v3-20261020-a. Applicant wallet: 0x037f58E33c1Ec8fdA272361E0aAC1e31054a1CDE. Contract version 1.3.1, StudioNet.

Three treasury numbers

The campaign pool is its funded GEN balance tracked by the contract. Reserved is the portion promised to eligible applicants that has not yet been withdrawn or released. Available is the remaining unreserved amount: pool minus reserved, clamped to zero.

An eligibility judgment does not create money. If the available pool cannot cover the configured reward, the application becomes ELIGIBLE_UNDERFUNDED. After the creator adds sufficient funds, anyone can call reserve_underfunded for that applicant. This deterministic action uses the recorded verdict; it does not ask the model to judge the contribution again.

A worked numerical example

The following figures are a hypothetical accounting example, not a claim that these transfers have already happened in this campaign. Suppose a creator funds 0.003 GEN and an eligible applicant reserves a reward of 0.001 GEN.

The creator must first make the campaign inactive before calling reclaim_unused_pool. That operation transfers only the available 0.002 GEN in this example. The reserved 0.001 GEN stays protected and remains withdrawable by its applicant. Reclaim does not cancel an existing reservation.

What if the applicant never withdraws?

A reservation becomes release-eligible at 00:00 UTC on the calendar date thirty days after its reservation date. This is a date-based rule, not necessarily 720 elapsed hours. Anyone can then call release_expired_reservation. Release clears that applicant's pending reward and reduces reserved by the same amount; pool does not change. No GEN is transferred by the release itself.

Release is not automatic. Until a release transaction executes, an overdue applicant can still withdraw. The first successful withdrawal or release makes the other operation unavailable. Released value becomes available to the campaign; only the creator may reclaim it, and only while the campaign is inactive.

Evidence and execution

Eligibility is a bounded validator judgment about the published contribution. Pool accounting, roles and settlement are deterministic. A finalized transaction label alone does not prove successful execution or native delivery: inspect the leader execution, resulting state and actual transfer when value moves. This note explains the mechanism and does not claim a completed runtime test.
